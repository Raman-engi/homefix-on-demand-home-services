# 🌟 Project Overview

HomeFix is a full-stack MERN-based home service marketplace designed to bridge the gap between customers and trusted service professionals.

The platform allows users to discover verified professionals, compare ratings, schedule bookings, manage services, track requests, and access customer support through a modern and responsive interface.

HomeFix includes dedicated dashboards for **Users**, **Professionals**, and **Administrators**, making it a complete home service management ecosystem.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Finding trusted home service professionals remains a challenge for many households.

Customers often rely on local contacts or unverified workers for services such as:

- Electrical Repair
- Plumbing
- AC Maintenance
- Cleaning Services
- Painting Services
- Appliance Repair

This creates several issues:

- Lack of trust and verification
- No transparent pricing system
- Difficult booking process
- Limited customer support
- No booking tracking mechanism
- Inconsistent service quality

As a result, customers frequently face delays, uncertainty, and poor service experiences.

---

# 💡 Solution

HomeFix provides a centralized digital platform where customers can discover, compare, and book verified professionals with confidence.

### The platform offers:

- ✅ Verified Service Professionals
- ✅ Transparent Pricing
- ✅ Simple Booking Workflow
- ✅ Booking Tracking System
- ✅ Multiple Payment Methods
- ✅ Dedicated Customer Support
- ✅ Professional Dashboard
- ✅ Admin Dashboard

This creates a reliable, scalable, and customer-friendly home services ecosystem.
